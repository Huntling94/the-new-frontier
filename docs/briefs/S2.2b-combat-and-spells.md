# Brief S2.2b — Combat and spells

**Status:** closed 7 Nov 2026.
**Read first:** `STATE.md`, `ARCHITECTURE.md`, D-001, D-004, D-005, D-008, D-015, D-018, D-019.
**Depends on:** S2.2a closed.
**Goal:** in the viewer, the squad fights a training dummy that hits back, and casts all nine spells.

## Why now

S2.2a gives the squad time, replays and movement, but nothing to do. This brief adds the character side of the game. S2.2c then adds what they fight.

The squad test is the reference for behaviour and values, and the maintainer supplies it.

## Decisions

1. **The dummy exists so that every rule can run.** The squad test has no dummy. It belongs to a `dummy` scenario (S2.2a), at a place in the data clear of camp. It stands still, is hostile from the start, strikes the nearest living unit in reach on a timer through the normal damage path, gives XP when it dies, and respawns after a delay. Its stats are plain data, copied from a level 5 Tide Crab with far more health and a longer reach of 5.5. A crab walks in to hit, and the dummy stands still, so a crab's reach of 3.8 would never touch a Fighter standing at the edge of melee range. `Scenario` gains `dummy`, a point or null, and only this field changes that exported type. The stats are not balance.
2. **Commands added:**

   | Command | Carries |
   |---|---|
   | `target` | Unit IDs, an enemy ID |
   | `cast` | Unit ID, spell, and an optional world point and enemy ID |
   | `potion` | An optional unit ID |
   | `upgrade` | Unit ID, gear slot |

3. **The core resolves aiming, as the squad test does.** Single-target shots use the enemy named, then the nearest within 12 of the point, then the shared target, then the unit's target, then the nearest enemy in range. Volley uses the point, then the caster. Mend uses the wounded ally nearest the point, then the lowest-health ally. The shared target is a rule value and lives in state. Selection does not.
4. **Commands that cannot happen are refused** with a reason in an event: on cooldown, no mana, no target, caster down, not that unit's spell, no potions, nobody hurt, no marks, not near the anvil, or already +10. Revive with nobody down has no target.
5. **New events:** `hit`, `healed`, `spell-cast`, `level-up`, `unit-down`, `enemy-down`, `unit-revived`, `upgraded`, `item-destroyed` and `squad-down`. S2.3 uses them for damage text and sound.
6. **Delayed effects are scheduled records** (D-015). Volley's three strikes are entries in state, not functions.
7. **Shots are state.** Shooter attacks and shots are homing projectiles with a speed and a lifetime, as in the squad test.
8. **Combat maths carries every term:** attack modifier, the spell's multiplier, crit ×2, Rally ×1.3, stagger ×1.5, AR against DR, Def, and a minimum of 1. Incoming damage adds Guard ×0.3 and invulnerability frames.
9. **All three down raises `squad-down` and stops the run.** The tick keeps counting and nothing else changes, because replays and the viewer rely on the tick rising. Later commands are refused with `no-living-units`. Wipes and the return to camp come in S2.2c.
10. **Taunt, the slow and knockback set their values here,** and this brief tests only that they are set. Their effects act on monster behaviour, so S2.2c tests those.
11. **`upgradeOffer(state, unitId, slot)` gives an upgrade's odds and cost.** It joins the core's public interface. The roll and S2.3's anvil screen both use it, so what is shown is what is rolled.
12. **The anvil is a place.** An `upgrade` command is refused unless a living character is near it, as in the squad test, so any of the twelve slots can be upgraded from there, a downed character's included. `isNearAnvil(state)` joins the public interface for S2.3's prompt. Pausing for the anvil screen belongs to `packages/app`, and the command lands through `applyCommands`.
13. **Hit-stop is visual only.** The squad test slows its rules for a moment on a crit. Here the rules run on, and S2.3 handles the pause on screen.
14. **Tests may set state directly** to reach a case. Starting marks are 450, reaching +5 costs over 2,000, and loot arrives in S2.2c. Replays cannot set state, so the spell replay reaches Revive by moving the Healer out of range until the Fighter falls.
15. **Data stays separate** (D-018). Classes, spells, upgrade tables, the combat maths' constants, timings and ranges all live in `packages/core/data`. Spells run through a plain `switch`, with no spell engine until something needs one (D-019).
16. **Replays are re-recorded on purpose** when this brief changes their hashes (S2.2a). The squad tour's final positions must not change.
17. **A lone Healer stays put.** In the squad test, a Healer with no living allies walks to the island's centre when an enemy is near. That is a bug, and it is not carried over.
18. **The viewer plays a run close to CI's, not the same run.** Browsers round slightly differently (S2.2a), and combat can turn that into a shifted hit or dice roll. The spell replay leaves wide margins, and a headless Chromium run confirms all nine spells fire there.

## Scope

**In:**

- The training dummy.
- Unit behaviour: auto-attacking, shots and the Healer's single-target auto heal (D-008).
- Targeting, and attack-move's effect.
- All nine spells, cooldowns and mana.
- Damage in both directions, going down and Revive.
- Levels, and XP split among the living (D-004).
- Twelve gear slots (four per character), and upgrades (D-005).
- Potions.
- The viewer extended to show health, the dummy, shots and spell effects, with a choice of replay.
- One more hand-written replay that fires all nine spells.

**Out:**

- Monsters, the boss, loot and wipes (S2.2c).
- Auto-combat and bots (S2.2c).
- 3D drawing, audio, HUD and browser input (S2.3).
- Enchant chips, stances, the Family House and saves (S4).
- Benched XP, which needs the Family House (S4).
- Other classes (S4).

## Done means

1. `pnpm check` passes, with unit tests covering damage maths, XP splitting, upgrade odds and each spell.
2. Two runs with the same seed and commands end with an identical state hash.
3. Both replays end on their `expectedHash` on CI with no `order-refused`, the spell replay's events show all nine spells cast, and the mid-run save test covers combat.
4. **The `ge.c` bugs that apply here have named regression tests:**
   - Upgrade odds never rise as upgrade level rises, and fall past +4.
   - The odds offered match the odds rolled.
   - More accuracy never reduces damage.
   - Very low accuracy never gives negative damage.
   - Paying for an upgrade actually takes the money.
5. A check on the tracked files finds no secrets, local paths or unintended personal information.
6. The maintainer has approved the exact file list and diff before any commit.
7. CI is green on `master` after the maintainer's approved push.
8. The maintainer has watched a replay in the viewer: the squad fights the dummy, takes damage, and all nine spells fire.

## Stop and ask if

- A squad-test behaviour cannot be matched inside the fixed tick.
- A rule cannot be expressed without the cursor, camera or selection.
- The core's public interface needs a change this brief does not name.
- Any standing trigger in `CLAUDE.md` fires.

## Outcome (builder fills in)

All eight checks pass. CI was green on `master` at `40af80c`, and the deploy followed. The spell tour ends on `b671ab56` and the squad tour on `f636a152`, on both Windows and CI. All nine spells fire with no `order-refused`, and the save test now also covers shots and strikes in the air. The maintainer approved the run and the diff.

The squad tour's positions match the old build at every tick, so its new hash comes only from state growing.

Each protection was shown to fail when it should. In a throwaway clone, 115 deliberate breakages of the maths, upgrades, spells, aiming, behaviour, saves and replays were tried. 113 turned tests red at once. One exposed a weak test, now fixed. The other removed a guard with no effect of its own, because an empty average is NaN and the "too far" check is then false. The Healer still stays put, and the bug the guard stands against, walking to the island's centre, now fails a test.

The public interface gained only `upgradeOffer` and `isNearAnvil(state)`, plus the `Scenario.dummy` field. No squad-test behaviour needed a stop.

**The viewer in Chromium:** decision 18 expected drift. There was none in this replay's key events: all nine spells, the Fighter's fall, Revive and the dummy's death land on the same ticks as in Node, and the same final health. Only the hash differs. Margins stay in the replay, because this is one result and not a promise.

**Departures:**

- The dummy strikes every second, with health 34,000. Both suit the replay, and neither is balance.
- Refusal names beyond those in decision 4 are `already-max`, and `no-living-units` for a potion named on a downed unit.
- A named enemy that is down falls through to the next aiming step, rather than refusing.
- The dummy keeps its ID when it respawns, so a hand-written replay can name it throughout.
- The anvil stands at the waystone, as in the squad test, with a reach of 10.
- `packages/core/test/helpers.ts` holds shared test helpers.
- The S2.2a test that lists every timing grew to list the new ones.
- `ARCHITECTURE.md` gains three lines on tick order, delayed effects and aiming.

**Found while building:** a unit sent alone on attack-move is also set to hold, so a Fighter stops about 8 from an enemy and never closes. It comes from the squad test, so it is kept here, and it is noted in `STATE.md`.
