# Brief S2.2c — Monsters, boss and bots

**Status:** closed 7 Nov 2026.
**Read first:** `STATE.md`, `ARCHITECTURE.md`, D-004, D-007, D-009, D-015, D-018, D-019.
**Depends on:** S2.2b closed.
**Goal:** a complete squad-test run plays with no browser, from the first camp to the boss, and auto-combat clears the island.

## Why now

S2.2a and S2.2b give the squad its rules, but only a dummy to fight. This brief completes the game's rules and adds the bot, so that every later change can be checked by playing the game.

The squad test is the reference for behaviour and values, and the maintainer supplies it.

## Decisions

1. **The boss starts from the squad-test values:** health 32,000 and base attack 215.
2. **Balance is out of scope.** There are no win-rate targets. The balance report exists to spot change, and the maintainer's own play decides difficulty.
3. **Bots are players.** They act only through commands, and walk to the anvil to upgrade. The squad test's bot changes state directly, and that is not carried over.
4. **Auto-combat and the bot share their roaming** (D-007). Auto-combat stops at bosses, as ruled. The bot adds a boss layer on top, in careful and sloppy versions.
5. **A finished run** means the boss died within a tick limit. The report also counts wipes and characters down.
6. **Packs do not respawn.** The squad test's respawn code never runs, so it is not ported. Zone respawn rules come with S3.
7. **Telegraphs are scheduled records** (D-015), with their delays in ticks.
8. **The real game starts from an `island` scenario** (S2.2a), with the seven packs and no dummy. Earlier replays keep their own scenarios, so packs do not change them.
9. **Auto-combat and bots are named exports.** `autoCommands(view)` returns `{ commands, stop }`, where `stop` is a stated reason or null. `botCommands(view, kind)` returns commands. They use no randomness and keep no memory, so what they do depends only on the state they read. They think every 0.5 and 0.2 seconds, as in the squad test. `tools` gains its dependency on `core` for the runner.
10. **Data stays separate** (D-018). Monster types, packs, the boss and its moves, loot tables and bot thresholds all live in `packages/core/data`. A new monster or pack should need data only, unless it has a new behaviour.
11. **Wipes replace decision 9 of S2.2b, in every scenario.** After `squad-down` the rules wait 2.2 seconds, and commands are still refused. Then the squad returns to camp with full health and mana and no cooldowns, and the boss resets and loses its minions. Marks, XP and damage already done to monsters all stay, as in the squad test.
12. **The lone attack-move bug is fixed here.** An attack-move that picks a target sets `forced`, the flag the `target` command uses, so a holding unit closes on it. Testing `attackMove` itself would fail, because a chasing unit stops getting nearer its destination and gives up after 0.7 seconds.
13. **Only a hit on the boss interrupts it.** In the squad test, Crippling Shot interrupts the boss's cast whatever it hits. That is a bug, and it is not carried over.
14. **Enemies carry their own stats** in state, because level scales them. The dummy keeps its behaviour, and the replays' events must not change. Their hashes change because state grows, and they are re-recorded on purpose.
15. **Units leave sleeping monsters alone unless told.** Idle units fight only awake enemies. Attack-move may start a fight with a sleeping pack, but not with a sleeping boss, as in the squad test.
16. **A stall** is 60 game seconds with no kill, no loot and no change in anyone's health. The stall test runs 100 seeds, because a 3-in-100 fault usually passes five. If that does not fit the 60-second job, the maintainer decides.
17. **The tick limit** is 36,000 ticks, 20 game minutes, unless a clean run needs longer.
18. **The viewer gains a live bot.** It runs `botCommands` in the page for a chosen seed, because a recorded run can drift in Chromium and a live bot reacts to whatever happens. It joins the replay picker, with a faster speed.
19. **The public interface changes,** and only as follows:
    - `Scenario` gains `packs` and `boss`.
    - `ISLAND_SCENARIO`, `autoCommands`, `botCommands` and their types join the exports.
    - `RefusalReason` gains `run-over`.
    - `GameEvent` gains `boss-woken`, `interrupted`, `loot`, `squad-returned` and `victory`.
20. **Obstacles keep a gap of 1.8** (agreed while building). Random placement left about 20 pairs per island too close for a unit to pass, and a walking unit wedged between them for good. This was the stall in the squad test. It changes S2.2a's world, so both replays' positions move, and they are re-recorded on purpose. Real pathfinding is a later decision.
21. **The squad counts as fighting only while a unit that can fight has an awake monster in range.** In the squad test it was any awake monster within 24 of the squad's centre, which could leave everyone waiting on a monster nobody would engage.

## Scope

**In:**

- Monsters: the seven packs, aggro, and their four behaviours.
- Loot: marks and potion drops, and collecting them.
- The boss's four moves, melee, telegraph timing, interrupts and enrage.
- Wipes and the return to camp.
- Victory.
- Auto-combat (D-007).
- Careful and sloppy bots.
- A runner in `packages/tools`, run through `tsx`: `pnpm sim --seed <n> --bot careful|sloppy`.
- A report across many seeds, `pnpm sim:report`, printed to the terminal with no stored baseline.
- The viewer extended to show monsters and telegraphs.

**Out:**

- 3D drawing, audio, HUD and browser input (S2.3).
- Balance work (no brief yet).
- New regions, dungeons, monster pools and the world validator (S3).

## Done means

1. `pnpm check` passes, with unit tests covering aggro, interrupts, enrage, XP on kills and wipes, plus what Taunt, the slow and knockback do to monsters. A save taken in the middle of a boss fight, with telegraphs, monster shots and drops about, plays on identically. The lone attack-move bug has a regression test.
2. Both bots finish a run on five seeds in CI, and that job takes under 60 seconds.
3. Auto-combat clears the island or stops for a stated reason on each of 100 seeds. A stall fails the test, because the squad test stalled in 3 of 100 runs.
4. Two runs with the same seed end with an identical state hash.
5. `packages/core` still passes the lint rules from S2.1.
6. A check on the tracked files finds no secrets, local paths or unintended personal information.
7. The maintainer has approved the exact file list and diff before any commit.
8. CI is green on `master` after the maintainer's approved push.
9. The maintainer has seen a bot run in the terminal, the report, and a boss fight in the viewer.

## Stop and ask if

- A bot cannot finish a run without changing a rule in the register. This includes a sloppy bot that cannot kill the boss. Seeds are not hand-picked to hide it.
- The core's public interface needs a change this brief does not name.
- Any standing trigger in `CLAUDE.md` fires.

## Outcome (builder fills in)

All nine checks pass. CI was green on `master` at `1e480f0`, and the deploy followed. The bot runs and the 100-seed stall test take 23 seconds on CI, inside `pnpm check`, so `ci.yml` did not change. The spell tour ends on `903a5150` and the squad tour on `341096b5`. The maintainer saw a bot run, the report and a boss fight, and approved the diff.

**The stall in the squad test is found and fixed.** It had two causes. Random placement left about 20 pairs of obstacles per island too close for a unit to pass, and a walking unit wedged between them for good. Auto-combat also waited on any awake monster near the squad's centre, even one nobody would engage. Decisions 20 and 21 fix them. Before the fixes 2 of 20 bot runs failed. After them, 100 of 100 finished for each bot, and auto-combat cleared the island on all 100 seeds.

**Replays:** with everything in except the obstacle gap, both replays matched the old build on every tick for events, positions, health, XP, the dice and marks. Only the gap moved them.

Each protection was shown to fail when it should. In a throwaway clone, 77 deliberate breakages were tried. 65 turned tests red at once. The other 12 exposed weak tests, now fixed, and all 12 then failed.

**The viewer in Chromium:** both bots finished five seeds each in the page, using all four boss moves, with no refused orders.

**Found while building:** the bots never struggle. Across 100 seeds there were no wipes and no characters down, and the boss dies about 2.6 game minutes into a run, even to the sloppy bot. Decision 2 puts balance out of scope, so nothing changed. It means wipes are proven by unit tests, not by a bot run.

**Departures:**

- Decisions 20 and 21 were agreed while building.
- Telegraphs for a Slag Golem's slam land where they were drawn. The squad test uses the Golem's position when the wind-up ends.
- A drop flies for a fixed time, and the core keeps no height for it.
- The boss leaves no loot, because the run ends when it dies.
- Dead monsters leave `state.enemies` at the end of the tick. The dummy stays, because it respawns.
- A path to a point inside the arena now goes straight there. In the squad test it swung out to the same detour point for ever.
- Bots walk to the boss from the camp side, by way of `bossApproach`, so they do not walk straight into the beacon.
- `packages/tools` exports `play`, so a test can prove a stall is noticed.
- Zone-aware enemies were not added. They went to the design session, with pathfinding and save versions.
