# Brief S2.3b — Interface and feel

**Status:** closed 7 Nov 2026. Agreed the same day, amended for D-031, D-034 and D-035 after checking against S2.3a's code, and changed by D-036 and D-037 after the maintainer's play.
**Read first:** `STATE.md`, `ARCHITECTURE.md`, `docs/DATA-MODEL.md`, brief S2.3a, D-003, D-005, D-007, D-013, D-017, D-019, D-025, D-027, D-028, D-029, D-031, D-034, D-035.
**Depends on:** S2.3a closed.
**Goal:** the rebuilt game plays and feels the same as the approved squad test, from the start screen to victory.

## Why now

S2.3a makes the squad visible and commandable. A player still cannot see cooldowns, upgrade gear, hear a hit or start a run. This brief adds the interface and the feel, so the game can be played from start to finish and the public page can go live.

The squad test is the reference for side-by-side comparison, and the maintainer supplies it.

## Decisions

1. **The core stays in charge,** as in S2.3a. Screens read state and events, and act only through commands.
2. **Hit-stop slows the clock, not the rules.** On a crit, the app runs ticks at 15% speed for 0.05 seconds, as the squad test does, the same way slow time works. The core never knows, so runs stay repeatable (S2.2b). Freezing only the picture would make it jump to catch up.
3. **Clicks answer at once.** Click sounds play on the press, not on the next tick.
4. **The anvil pauses the game.** Ticks stop while it is open, and its commands still land through `applyCommands` (S2.2a).
5. **The anvil lists the slots the character's gear holds,** not a fixed four (D-028). Its odds and costs come from `upgradeOffer` (D-005). The core's slots stay four until S3, so the test in done means 3 uses a made-up character, and the core does not change.
6. **HUD cards follow the characters,** one per character in its place in the active three, never one per class (D-025). Each card shows whether that character is in auto-combat (D-035).
7. **Spell buttons come from data,** in the same order as the spell keys (D-027, D-031).
8. **Names and labels come from the lookup in `render`** from S2.3a (D-029). It gains spell and gear slot names.
9. **Fonts are bundled** with the game, not loaded from Google, and listed in `NOTICE`.
10. **Sound is synthesised,** with a mute control on `N` (D-013). There is no background drone or music (S5).
11. **The README links to the public page** when this brief closes, because the game is then playable (D-017).
12. **The controls are D-003 and D-013, with the changes in D-031, D-034 and D-035.** The anvil is on `Y`. The squad test's mouse and close keys carry over: clicking a card selects that character, clicking a spell button casts it, and `Esc` or `Y` closes the anvil. These add to the controls without changing any, and are recorded in the Outcome.
13. **The HUD replaces S2.3a's stopgaps.** The boss's overhead health bar and the "Auto-combat" tag leave `render`.
14. **The camera shakes on big hits,** with the squad test's values: 0.3 when a character is hurt or a brute slams, 0.7 when a slam lands, 1 when the boss wakes or blasts, and 0.5 on an interrupt, fading at 2.4 a second. It lives in `packages/app` and stays off when the system asks for reduced motion.
15. **The core gains two reads for the HUD,** and nothing else: a character's card values (maximum mana, XP to the next level) and a spell's cooldown and mana cost, plus the potion cooldown. They only add to the interface, so replays do not change.

## Scope

**In:**

- HUD: a card for each character with its auto-combat state, spell buttons, cooldowns, the boss bar, the cast bar, the log and help.
- HUD as in the squad test: marks, potions, the slow-time meter and its screen border, the run clock, the objective line, banners and the anvil prompt.
- Floating damage text.
- Screens: start, anvil and victory. A wipe shows as a banner while the core returns the squad to camp, as in the squad test.
- Hit-stop and camera shake.
- Synthesised sound and a mute control.
- Pause when the anvil is open.
- The README link to the public page.

**Out:**

- Saves (S3, D-024).
- More gear slots, item types and the store (S3, D-025, D-028).
- Art, models, music and background sound (S5).
- Cities, NPCs, shops, inventory, quests and a second zone (S3).
- Keyboard rebinding (no brief yet).
- Balance work (no brief yet).
- The finished public page (S7).

## Done means

1. `pnpm check` passes.
2. `pnpm test:e2e` covers these flows in a real browser:
   - Start a run from the start screen.
   - Cast a spell from its button, and see its cooldown begin.
   - Open the anvil, buy an upgrade, and see the game pause and resume.
   - Mute and unmute the sound.
3. A test proves the anvil shows every slot it is given, using a made-up character with D-028's seven slots.
4. A full bot run from start to victory in the real page raises no console errors.
5. `docs/DATA-MODEL.md` is updated if an entity, relationship or rule changed.
6. Frame rate is measured in the boss fight with the display's frame limit off, on the same seed, for S2.3a (`88053ae`) and this build, and recorded here.
7. A check on the tracked files and built site finds no secrets, local paths or unintended personal information.
8. The maintainer has approved the exact file list and diff before any commit.
9. CI is green on `master` after the maintainer's approved push.
10. **The maintainer has played it beside the squad test,** from the start screen to victory, including the whole boss fight. Tests alone cannot close this brief, and it stays open until all four of the squad test's questions are answered:
    - Did you feel like you were commanding a team, or babysitting one?
    - Did losing the Healer change how you played?
    - Could you follow all three during the boss? S2.3a left this unanswered.
    - Did the camera do what your hands expected?

## Stop and ask if

- Any control has to behave differently from D-003, D-013, D-031, D-034 or D-035.
- This build's slowest 5% of frames in the boss fight take more than 20% longer than S2.3a's, measured as in done means 6.
- The core needs a change beyond the reads in decision 15. Make it here only if its tests and replays still pass.
- Any standing trigger in `CLAUDE.md` fires.

## Outcome (builder fills in)

The game plays from the start screen to victory in the browser, with the squad test's HUD, screens, sound and feel. Every check passes in Chromium. The maintainer played it beside the squad test, asked for D-036 and D-037, and closed the brief as fine for now, to move on with the build.

**Agreed while checking the brief against the code:** the amendments above. Hit-stop slows the clock instead of freezing the picture, the HUD takes the squad test's full set, camera shake is in, the background drone is out, and the anvil's seven-slot test uses a made-up character.

**The core gained two reads and one rule change.**

- `cardOf(view, unitId)` gives a card's maximum mana and XP to the next level. `spellInfo(spell)` gives a spell's cooldown in ticks and its mana cost. Both only add to the interface. The potion cooldown named in decision 15 was not needed, so it was not added.
- **Auto-combat fights bosses (D-036).** `autoCommands` no longer stops with `boss`, and `AutoStop` lost that value. With the boss awake, it sends any character in it that is not fighting or walking at the boss. No replay or bot run changed, because neither uses auto-combat once the boss is awake. Handed the boss fight on ten seeds, auto-combat alone wins every one, with no wipes, in about 82 seconds.

**Controls added, as decision 12 allowed:**

| Input | Action |
|---|---|
| Click a card | Select that character |
| Click a spell button | Cast it, aimed by the core |
| `Esc`, or `Y` again | Close the anvil |

**Changed after the maintainer's play:**

- **Auto-combat no longer refuses while the boss is awake (D-036).**
- **The camera follows a character selected alone (D-037).** `F1` to `F3`, a click, a card or a box around one turns follow on, onto that character. More than one selected, or that character down, and it follows the squad.

**Frame rate in the boss fight (done means 6).** Measured in headless Chromium on an RTX 2080 Ti at 1280 by 720, seed 1, with the display's frame limit off, over 20 seconds once the boss wakes:

| Build | Slowest 5% of frames | Frames a second |
|---|---|---|
| S2.3a (`88053ae`), three runs | 2.3 to 3.0 ms | 450 to 580 |
| S2.3b | 3.9 to 4.0 ms | about 370 |
| S2.3b with its HUD hidden | 2.7 ms | about 475 |
| Squad test | 7.0 ms | about 200 |

The stop trigger fired: the slowest frames take about 60% longer than S2.3a's, against a limit of 20%. The cost is the HUD's page elements drawn over WebGL, and hiding them brings this build back to S2.3a's level. Writing only changed values and scaling bars instead of resizing them took 4.2 ms to 4.0. The maintainer accepted the cost, because the build is still faster than the squad test and far inside a 60 Hz frame. The trigger compared with a build that had no HUD, so later briefs should measure against a frame budget instead.

**Found while building:**

- The app names Crippling Shot as the interrupting spell on the cast bar, because the core does not say which spells interrupt.
- Rally and the boss's stagger are logged without their percentages, so the log cannot drift from the data.
- Crippling Shot's "SLOWED" text from the squad test is not shown, because no event reports a slow.
- Fonts come from the Fontsource packages, with only their Latin subsets loaded.
- Port 5173, Vite's default, may be taken by another local project. `pnpm dev --port 5174` avoids it.

**The maintainer's play beside the squad test:**

- **Camera shake, the boss fight, and spell buttons and keys:** "all fine for now".
- **Could you follow all three during the boss?** Fine for now, with the camera following one character selected alone (D-037).
- **Did the camera do what your hands expected?** Fine for now, with D-037 added.
- **Commanding a team, or babysitting one?** and **Did losing the Healer change how you played?** Not answered separately. The maintainer closed the brief to keep the build moving, and will come back to the feel later.
