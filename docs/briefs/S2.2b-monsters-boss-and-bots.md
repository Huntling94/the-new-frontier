# Brief S2.2b — Monsters, boss and bots

**Status:** draft.
**Read first:** `STATE.md`, `ARCHITECTURE.md`, D-004, D-007, D-009, D-015, D-018.
**Depends on:** S2.2a closed.
**Goal:** a complete squad-test run plays with no browser, from the first camp to the boss, and auto-combat clears the island.

## Why now

S2.2a gives the squad rules but nothing to fight. This brief completes the game's rules and adds the bot, so that every later change can be checked by playing the game.

The squad test is the reference for behaviour and values, and the maintainer supplies it.

## Decisions

1. **The boss starts from the squad-test values:** health 32,000 and base attack 215.
2. **Balance is out of scope.** There are no win-rate targets. The balance report exists to spot change, and the maintainer's own play decides difficulty.
3. **Bots are players.** They act only through commands, and walk to the anvil to upgrade. The squad test's bot changes state directly, and that is not carried over.
4. **Auto-combat and the bot share their roaming** (D-007). Auto-combat stops at bosses, as ruled. The bot adds a boss layer on top, in careful and sloppy versions.
5. **A finished run** means the boss died within a tick limit. The report also counts wipes and characters down.
6. **Packs do not respawn.** The squad test's respawn code never runs, so it is not ported. Zone respawn rules come with S3.
7. **Telegraphs are scheduled records** (D-015), with their delays in ticks.
8. **Data stays separate** (D-018). Monster types, packs, the boss and its moves, loot tables and bot thresholds all live in `packages/core/data`. A new monster or pack should need data only, unless it has a new behaviour.

## Scope

**In:**

- Monsters: the seven packs, aggro, and their four behaviours.
- Loot: marks and potion drops, and collecting them.
- The boss's four moves, melee, telegraph timing, interrupts and enrage.
- Wipes and the return to camp.
- Victory.
- Auto-combat (D-007).
- Careful and sloppy bots.
- A runner in `packages/tools`, run through `tsx`: `pnpm sim --seed <n> --bot careful|sloppy`.
- A report across many seeds.
- The viewer from S2.2a extended to show monsters and telegraphs.

**Out:**

- 3D drawing, audio, HUD and browser input (S2.3).
- Balance work (no brief yet).
- New regions, dungeons, monster pools and the world validator (S3).

## Done means

1. `pnpm check` passes, with unit tests covering aggro, interrupts, enrage, XP on kills and wipes.
2. Both bots finish a run on five seeds in CI, and that job takes under 60 seconds.
3. Auto-combat clears the island or stops for a stated reason on every seed. A stall fails the test, because the squad test stalled in 3 of 100 runs.
4. Two runs with the same seed end with an identical state hash.
5. `packages/core` still passes the lint rules from S2.1.
6. A check on the tracked files finds no secrets, local paths or unintended personal information.
7. The maintainer has approved the exact file list and diff before any commit.
8. CI is green on `master` after the maintainer's approved push.
9. The maintainer has seen a bot run in the terminal, the report, and a boss fight in the viewer.

## Stop and ask if

- A bot cannot finish a run without changing a rule in the register.
- Any standing trigger in `CLAUDE.md` fires.
