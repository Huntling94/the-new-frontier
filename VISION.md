# Vision

A free, open-source 3D squad RPG. You command a family of three at once and help the frontier: levelling up, doing quests and reaching new cities.

1. **Three bodies, one player.** They fight by themselves. You decide where they stand and when to spend their spells.
2. **A family you shape.** Five classes, three at once, with others rotated through the Family House.
3. **Levels and gear that make you choose.** XP splits among the living. Many gear slots, never enough money, and upgrades that can destroy what you love.
4. **A frontier that opens up.** Safe cities with NPCs and shops. Unsafe zones and dungeons, each with their own monsters, drops and levels.
5. **No grind.** `Space` handles what is dull. You handle what matters.

## The end state, v1.0

- Several cities, each with the zones and dungeons around them.
- Quests, inventory, items and NPC shops.
- Five classes with two stances each.
- A public page where anyone can play in the browser.
- A repository whose briefs, decisions and CI show how it was made.
